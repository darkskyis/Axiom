Tax

What to Do After Receiving an FBR Notice

An FBR notice should be handled through documents, deadlines, and a clear response strategy. A hurried informal reply can create avoidable tax exposure.

1. Confirm the notice typeIdentify whether the notice relates to audit, assessment, withholding, income source, sales tax, property, or documentation.
2. Calendar the deadlineMissing a response date can narrow options for explanation, compliance, appeal, or settlement strategy.
3. Preserve recordsKeep returns, wealth statements, bank records, invoices, property papers, tax challans, and correspondence together.
4. Review before replyingHave counsel assess the legal position before submitting explanations or admitting facts.

Compliance

SECP Compliance Checklist for Growing Companies

Strong corporate compliance protects directors, investors, shareholders, and family businesses. The following checklist helps companies identify common SECP and governance gaps before they become regulatory or shareholder disputes.

Corporate recordsMaintain updated incorporation documents, Form 29, Form A, registers, shareholding records, and board resolutions.
Annual filingsTrack filing deadlines, annual returns, financial statements, auditor appointments, and statutory meeting records.
Director changesDocument resignations, appointments, share transfers, beneficial ownership, and authority to sign contracts.
Governance controlsKeep board approvals, shareholder approvals, related-party decisions, and major commercial commitments properly recorded.
Licensing and sector rulesReview whether the business requires sector-specific registrations, renewals, reporting, or regulator-facing documents.
Dispute readinessOrganize records so the company can respond quickly to SECP notices, shareholder questions, audits, or litigation.

Corporate

Warning Signs in a Shareholder Dispute

Shareholder disputes often begin quietly: information stops flowing, approvals become unclear, and business decisions are made without proper authority.

  • Financial records or bank information are withheld from a shareholder or director.
  • Board meetings, notices, or resolutions are disputed.
  • Share transfers, investment terms, or family-business understandings are undocumented.
  • One side controls operations while the other carries financial or reputational risk.
  • Settlement discussions begin without a clear litigation or documentation strategy.

Early legal review can preserve evidence, clarify authority, and reduce the risk of a corporate dispute becoming more expensive than necessary.

Tax Appeals

Understanding the Tax Appeal Process in Pakistan

A tax appeal requires careful review of the assessment order, legal grounds, documents, deadlines, and forum strategy. The strength of the appeal often depends on the record prepared before filing.

  • Identify the order being challenged and the applicable limitation period.
  • Separate factual disputes from legal grounds.
  • Compile tax returns, notices, replies, challans, bank records, and supporting documents.
  • Assess whether rectification, appeal, stay, or settlement strategy is appropriate.
  • Prepare for hearings with a clear written position and document index.

Private Client

Privacy in Family and Inheritance Disputes

Family and inheritance matters require a careful balance between legal action and private resolution. The goal is to protect rights without creating unnecessary exposure.

  • Preserve marriage, custody, maintenance, succession, and property documents.
  • Avoid public accusations before legal strategy is assessed.
  • Separate settlement issues from court issues.
  • Identify whether overseas family members or property holders need representation.
  • Use confidential consultation before filing or responding to proceedings.
